==================================================
1. 核心原则
==================================================

不要把：

fixed annual EOR O&M

全部除以CO2流量并塞进：

utilization_cost_per_ton。

正式映射分成：

A. EOR fixed OPEX
→ utilization_storage.fixed_opex

B. EOR variable OPEX
→ utilization_storage.utilization_cost_per_ton × q_u

原因：

当前模型已经显式包含：

fixed_opex * x_U
+
utilization_cost_per_ton * q_u
+
storage_cost_per_ton * q_s

因此没有必要为了降维而丢失固定成本的经济性质。

==================================================

如果：

如果：

EOR fixed OPEX

每年发生，

这代表：

已经建设好的EOR/下游设施需要维护、人员、监测、

Variable EOR OPEX：

只有：

q_u > 0

时才随EOR路径的实际新CO2流量发生。

28. 论文解释
==================================================

正确表述：

"EOR operating costs are mapped using a two-part
approximation. Reference-project fixed operating expenses are
linearly scaled to the model's EOR-throughput scale and
retained as annual fixed OPEX after downstream investment,
whereas flow-dependent injection, recycling and oil-production
expenses are aggregated over the reference lifecycle and
expressed per tonne of fresh CO2 routed to EOR."

并补：

"This mapping is a transparent model assumption rather than
an empirical claim that fixed EOR costs are perfectly linear
in throughput."
