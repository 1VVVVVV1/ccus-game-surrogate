15. 方法文稿表述
==================================================

正式写法：

"To avoid imposing an unnecessarily detailed annual reservoir
trajectory on the reduced-form stochastic game, recycled CO2
is represented by a stationary lifecycle-average recycle ratio.
One tonne of recycled CO2 is assumed per tonne of fresh CO2
routed to EOR. This approximation is consistent with
literature estimates in which recycled and net purchased CO2
are of similar lifecycle magnitude, while recognizing that
actual recycle rates vary over project life."
